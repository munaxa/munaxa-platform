## 2. Conditions attached to every acceptance

- **Accepted does not mean eliminated.** Every accepted risk is still technically present in AWS.
- **Testing only.** No acceptance carries over to production. Each one must be decided again before production.
- **No production data.** Testing holds no production or customer data. Nothing from the old Docs database or bucket is migrated (test data only).
- **No production secrets or signing keys** may be placed in Testing.
- **Isolation.** Nonprod stays isolated from prod: per-environment boundaries (H-1), the deny of the other environment, and separate roles. Today no production environment exists.
- **Production blockers.** R7, R19 and R20 are tracked as production blockers. The member-account architecture (R15) is a production prerequisite.
- **Out of scope.** CanteenHub was not inspected to check account contents. This is a known limit of the R1 to R6 facts [U].
